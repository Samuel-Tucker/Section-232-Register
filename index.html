Section 232 Invoice Register

Research each product you ship to the US once — then copy the verified Section 232 and metal-composition answers straight onto every commercial invoice. Everything stays in your browser: no account, nothing uploaded.

New here? How to research a product (5 steps)
STEP 1Classify itFind the 10-digit US HTS code. Your UK commodity code's first 6 digits carry over — finish the job at hts.usitc.gov, and check rulings on similar products at rulings.cbp.gov.
STEP 2Check the listsSearch your HTS code against the steel & aluminium derivative annexes (HTSUS Chapter 99 notes / cbp.gov guidance). Not listed → Section 232: No.
STEP 3Measure the metalManufacturer data sheet first; otherwise an honest estimate from component weights. Batteries, motors, screws, springs and fittings all count. If the product is caught, also ask your supplier where the steel was melted & poured (mill certificates). Write down how you got the numbers.
STEP 4Record it hereAdd the product below. From then on, dispatch just clicks to copy the verified answer — no re-researching, no guessing.
STEP 5Re-verifyThe lists change roughly three times a year. Entries verified more than 4 months ago get an amber "re-verify" badge as a nudge.
